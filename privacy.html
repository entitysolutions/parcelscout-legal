Privacy Policy

Effective July 29, 2026

ParcelScout is built so that your activity stays on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not run servers that store your data, and we do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

What stays on your device

  • Saved parcels, notes, tags, and pin colors. Stored locally on your iPhone. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Your settings and free-lookup count. Stored locally.

What leaves your device

  • Map coordinates. When you tap the map, search, or use your location to find the parcel you are standing on, the coordinate or search term is sent to the parcel-data service that answers the query. That is the only way to look up a property record. These requests are not tied to an account, a device identifier, or an advertising identifier.
  • Nothing else. We do not transmit your saved parcels, notes, contacts, photos, or any identifier.

Location

ParcelScout requests location only while you are using the app ("when in use"), and only to center the map on you and identify the parcel at your position. We do not track your location in the background, we do not build a location history, and we do not sell or share location data. You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings and still use every other feature — search, saved parcels, and tapping any parcel on the map.

Property records shown in the app

Parcel boundaries, owner names, mailing addresses, acreage, sale prices, and assessed values come from public records published by county and state government agencies, delivered through the data service selected in Settings. ParcelScout does not create, verify, or modify these records.

Because these are government public records, we cannot remove or correct an individual entry. If a record about you is wrong or you want it restricted, contact the county assessor or recorder that publishes it; corrections flow to us on the next data refresh. Some states redact owner names by law, so those fields may be blank.

Subscriptions

ParcelScout Pro is sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription is active. We never see your name, payment method, or Apple Account details.

Children

ParcelScout is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will appear here and in the app before the change takes effect.
