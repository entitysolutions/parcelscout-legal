Common questions

  • Are the parcel lines a survey? No. They come from county GIS records and are for reference only — accuracy varies by several feet or more. Only a licensed surveyor can locate a legal boundary.
  • Why is the owner out of date? Records reflect the county's last published refresh, which ranges from weeks to years. Every parcel shows its refresh age so you can judge it.
  • Why is my county missing? Coverage depends on what the county or state publishes. Email us and we'll tell you where it stands.
  • How do I cancel Pro? iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ParcelScout. The free daily lookups keep working afterward.
  • How do I remove my name from a record? We publish public county records and cannot edit them. Contact the county assessor or recorder; corrections reach us on the next refresh.
