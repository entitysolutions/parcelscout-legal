What ParcelScout is

ParcelScout displays property parcel boundaries and public property records on a map for informational and reference purposes only.

Parcel lines are not a survey

Do not rely on ParcelScout to locate a legal property boundary. Parcel geometry is digitized by county and state agencies from tax maps and varies in accuracy — commonly by several feet, and more in older or rural subdivisions. GPS adds its own error. Only a licensed surveyor can establish a boundary on the ground. Never site a fence, structure, excavation, harvest, or hunt based on what you see in this app, and always obtain permission before entering land you do not own.

Records may be out of date or incomplete

Owner names, addresses, sale figures, and valuations reflect the last refresh published by the responsible county or state agency. Refresh schedules differ by county — some are months or years behind. Every parcel in ParcelScout shows how old its data is so you can judge it. Coverage and available fields vary by county, and some areas are not covered at all. Data is provided "as is," without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose.
